How it works

Say it. Watch it happen. Or just do it yourself.

  1. Ask

    Say what you want in your own words. Attach a photo or a document if it helps. No commands to learn.

  2. Watch it work

    The agent writes code, runs it, installs what it needs, and fixes its own mistakes. All of it happens in a Linux system that lives on your phone, and you can see every step.

  3. Take the wheel

    Prefer to drive? Open the terminal. Same files, same tools, no limits. It is your pocket PC.

You already paid for all those cores and all that RAM. Might as well use all of it.

Phones are pocket PCs. Most days yours spends its power scrolling. Jasiri gives it a job.

Questions

Good questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. Jasiri is free for individuals, including for your own professional work. Teams and companies need a license. Get in touch and we will sort it out.

Do I need an AI account?

Yes, you bring your own key from OpenRouter, OpenAI, or any compatible service. Jasiri does not resell AI. You pay your provider directly and you choose the model.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 or later with a 64-bit processor. Recent phones feel best, especially for bigger jobs.

Is it on Google Play?

Not yet. That is the goal. Until then you install it directly from this site, and it checks for updates when you open it.

Does it work offline?

The Linux system and your files work without a connection. The agent needs to reach your AI provider. Running AI models on the phone itself is planned as a plugin.

What can the agent touch?

It works inside Jasiri's own space on your phone: the project folders and the Linux system. It cannot see your photos, contacts, or other apps. It can read and write the clipboard when it needs to. Because it can run commands and change files in its space, read what you ask it to do.

Where does my data go?

Your messages and any attachments go to the AI provider you picked, under that provider's terms. We run no servers that see your prompts or files. The privacy page has the details.

Can I request a feature or a plugin?

Please do. Email contact@benlester.me. Soon you will be able to describe what you want from inside the app.

Behind the build

The hard parts.

Jasiri took a lot of problems that do not show on a screenshot. A few of them:

  • A real Linux system inside an app that the store will accept.Phones have rules. We read them all.
  • An agent that works on your files without wrecking them.Power and caution pull in opposite directions.
  • Long jobs on a phone that wants to sleep.Android has opinions about background work.
  • A terminal that feels good on glass.Typing Ctrl-C on a touchscreen is a design problem.
