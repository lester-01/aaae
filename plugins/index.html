Plugins

One app. Then make it yours.

Plugins are one-tap setups that teach Jasiri a new trick. Install what you need, skip what you do not. A marketplace inside the app is on its way.

Power users

You do not need to wait.

Jasiri already comes with an AI agent and a full Linux system. If you can describe a setup, you can build it today. Plugins are the same building blocks, packaged for everyone else.

How we plan to do it

You decide what a plugin can see.

Plugins will ask before they touch your files or photos, and you choose which folders they may use. Nothing is read or changed until you say so. Anything that deletes or moves files is meant to ask first and keep an undo.
